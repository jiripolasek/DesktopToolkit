Last updated November 26, 2026.

This policy applies to **JPSoftworks Desktop Toolkit Gallery** for Windows, published by Jiri Polasek (Czechia).

## Summary

The Gallery runs on your device and does not require an account. It has no telemetry or analytics and does not send your data to the publisher or to third parties.

## Data on your device

The Gallery stores your preferences and diagnostic files, such as logs and crash reports, on your device. Diagnostic files can contain technical details such as the app version, timestamps, and error information, which may include file names or paths. Older log files are removed automatically, and you can delete any of these files at any time.

Some samples read information from Windows, such as system resource usage, to demonstrate controls. This information is only displayed and is not saved or sent anywhere.

## Information you share

Nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it. If you contact the publisher for support, for example by email or by sending diagnostic files, the publisher uses that information only to respond and troubleshoot. This processing is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in supporting the Gallery. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law. Email providers process messages to deliver and store them. The publisher may disclose information when required by law.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided to the publisher. Depending on applicable law, you may also request that processing be restricted, object to processing, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [support@jiripolasek.com](mailto:support@jiripolasek.com).
